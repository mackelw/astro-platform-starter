Returning to sport after injury is not a decision made on pain — it is made on measurement. We work in clear stages that are not skipped.

We begin by controlling swelling and pain and restoring range of motion, then rebuild strength and endurance, then train balance and joint position sense, and finally progress through the movements of your own sport: running, changing direction, jumping and landing.

We do not clear you for full return until you pass strength and side-to-side symmetry tests, because returning early is the single biggest cause of re-injury.

We work regularly with ankle sprains, hamstring strains, and shoulder injuries in throwing and swimming athletes.
