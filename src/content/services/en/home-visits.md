Some patients cannot travel to the centre, and for them a delay in starting rehabilitation is costly.

We deliver the session at home with the same content as a session here: assessment, manual therapy, exercise, and a home programme explained to whoever cares for the patient so the work continues between visits.

The service is aimed mainly at older adults, the period immediately after surgery, and stroke or hemiplegia cases that need frequent rehabilitation.

The number of weekly visits is agreed after the first assessment, and the price includes travel within the area near the centre.
