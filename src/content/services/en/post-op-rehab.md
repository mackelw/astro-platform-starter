After any joint or spinal operation, the final result is decided by the rehabilitation as much as by the surgery itself.

We follow your surgeon's protocol: how much range of motion is permitted each week, when weight-bearing is allowed, and when resistance work may begin. We neither progress fast enough to put the repair at risk, nor slowly enough to let the joint stiffen.

The usual stages are: control pain and swelling, restore range of motion, build strength, then return to daily or sporting activity.

We handle ACL reconstruction, knee and hip replacement, cartilage and shoulder procedures, and spinal fusion.
