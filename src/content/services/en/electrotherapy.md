Electrotherapy equipment is used here as an adjunct to manual therapy and exercise, never as a replacement for them.

Interferential current eases deep pain around a joint. Therapeutic ultrasound supports healing in soft tissue and tendons. Electrical stimulation re-activates a muscle weakened after surgery or a long period of immobilisation.

We choose the modality and its duration according to the stage of your condition: in the acute stage the aim is to reduce pain and inflammation, in the later stage it is to activate the muscle.

The session is short and is usually combined with manual therapy or exercise in the same visit.
