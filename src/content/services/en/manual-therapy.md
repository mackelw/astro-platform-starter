Manual therapy is the core of our work: techniques applied by hand directly to the joint, the muscle and the surrounding tissue.

It includes joint mobilisation to recover lost range, release of painful trigger points within the muscle, stretching of shortened tissue, and neuromuscular relaxation techniques.

It is usually used for conditions where restriction dominates over inflammation: frozen shoulder, a stiff neck, and mechanical low back pain.

This is not a relaxation massage — it is a targeted clinical procedure, and we measure its effect by range of motion before and after.
