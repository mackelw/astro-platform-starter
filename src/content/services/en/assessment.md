Your first visit is an assessment, not a treatment. We take a detailed history: when the pain started, what makes it better or worse, and whether an injury or operation came before it.

We then carry out a clinical examination — joint range of motion, muscle strength testing, condition-specific tests, and a pain score from zero to ten.

You leave with three clear things: a written diagnosis, a treatment plan with an expected number of sessions, and exercises to start at home the same day.

If your case needs imaging or an orthopaedic or neurological opinion before treatment begins, we say so plainly and refer you, rather than starting sessions that will not help you.
