title: "Quadriceps sets"
summary: "Activates the front thigh muscle without moving the knee — the first exercise after knee surgery."
bodyArea: "knee"
difficulty: "easy"
sets: 3
reps: 15
hold: "5s"
equipment: ["Towel"]
cautions: "Safe even in the first days after surgery unless your surgeon says otherwise."
order: 1
---

Lie or sit with your leg straight out in front of you.

Place a folded towel under your knee.

Press the back of your knee down into the towel while tightening the front thigh muscle, so the kneecap draws slightly upwards.

Hold five seconds, then relax. This exercise does not move the joint, so it is permitted very early, and it is essential for preventing muscle wasting after surgery or a cast.
