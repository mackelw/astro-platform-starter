---
title: "Pelvic tilt"
summary: "Activates the deep abdominal muscles and unloads the low back."
bodyArea: "back"
difficulty: "easy"
sets: 3
reps: 12
hold: "5s"
equipment: ["Mat"]
cautions: "A small movement — do not lift your buttocks off the floor."
order: 1
---

Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat on the floor.

Gently tighten your abdominal muscles and press your low back down towards the floor until the gap beneath it closes.

Hold for five seconds while breathing normally — do not hold your breath — then relax.

The movement is very small; if your buttocks lift off the floor, you are overdoing it.
