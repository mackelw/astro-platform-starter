title: "Chin tuck"
summary: "Corrects forward head posture and strengthens the deep neck flexors."
bodyArea: "neck"
difficulty: "easy"
sets: 3
reps: 10
hold: "5s"
equipment: []
cautions: "Stop if it causes dizziness or arm numbness."
order: 1
---

Sit or stand with a straight back and relaxed shoulders.

Draw your chin straight back in a horizontal line, as if making a "double chin" — without tipping your head up or down.

You should feel a gentle stretch at the back of the neck. Hold for five seconds, then release.

This is one of the most useful exercises for anyone at a screen all day. Repeat it every hour while working.
