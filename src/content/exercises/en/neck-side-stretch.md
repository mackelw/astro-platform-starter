---
title: "Neck side stretch"
summary: "Lengthens the upper trapezius and eases tension between neck and shoulder."
bodyArea: "neck"
difficulty: "easy"
sets: 2
reps: 3
hold: "20-30s"
equipment: []
cautions: "Gentle stretch only — never pull hard on your head."
order: 2
---

Sit tall and anchor your right hand under the seat or by your side to hold the shoulder down.

Gently tilt your head towards your left shoulder until you feel a stretch on the right side of the neck.

You may rest your left hand on your head to add a little more stretch — using the weight of the hand only, with no pulling.

Hold twenty to thirty seconds, then swap sides.
