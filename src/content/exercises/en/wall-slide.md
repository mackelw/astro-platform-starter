title: "Wall slide"
summary: "Gradually restores shoulder elevation, with the wall taking part of the load."
bodyArea: "shoulder"
difficulty: "medium"
sets: 3
reps: 10
hold: ""
equipment: ["Wall"]
cautions: "Go only as high as pain allows — never force the joint."
order: 2
---

Stand facing a wall, about a foot away from it.

Place your hand on the wall at waist height, then slide it slowly upwards as though wiping the wall.

The wall carries part of the arm's weight, so lifting is easier than raising it in the air.

Stop at the first point where pain begins, pause a moment, then lower slowly. Over the days you will notice your hand reaching higher.
