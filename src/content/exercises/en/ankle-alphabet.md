Sit on a chair and lift the affected foot slightly off the floor.

Imagine writing the letters of the alphabet in the air with your big toe, from A to Z.

All the movement comes from the ankle — your leg stays completely still.

This moves the ankle through every direction without having to remember separate exercises, and it is ideal in the first days after a sprain or once a cast is removed.
