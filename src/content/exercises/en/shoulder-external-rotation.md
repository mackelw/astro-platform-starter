---
title: "Shoulder external rotation"
summary: "Strengthens the rotator cuff muscles that stabilise the shoulder joint."
bodyArea: "shoulder"
difficulty: "medium"
sets: 3
reps: 12
hold: ""
equipment: ["Resistance band"]
cautions: "Keep your elbow tucked to your side — the movement comes from the shoulder, not the trunk."
order: 3
---

Anchor a resistance band at waist height and hold the end in your affected hand.

Stand with your elbow tucked against your side and bent to ninety degrees, forearm across your stomach.

Rotate your forearm outwards, away from your body, keeping the elbow exactly where it is — tuck a folded towel under the elbow to help hold it in place.

Return slowly. The movement is small but it is one of the most important shoulder stability exercises.
