---
title: "Pendulum exercise"
summary: "The first exercise after a shoulder injury or operation: it moves the joint without working the muscles."
bodyArea: "shoulder"
difficulty: "easy"
sets: 3
reps: 10
hold: ""
equipment: []
cautions: "The arm must hang completely relaxed — the movement comes from your body, not your shoulder."
order: 1
---

Stand and lean forwards slightly, supporting yourself on a table or chair back with your good hand.

Let the injured arm hang down, completely relaxed.

Move your body — not your shoulder — in small circles, so the arm follows under its own weight. Ten circles in each direction.

Repeat forwards and backwards, then side to side. This is very safe in the early stages because it requires no shoulder muscle contraction.
