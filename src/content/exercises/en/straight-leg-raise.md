---
title: "Straight leg raise"
summary: "Strengthens the quadriceps under greater load while the knee stays locked."
bodyArea: "knee"
difficulty: "medium"
sets: 3
reps: 12
hold: "3s"
equipment: []
cautions: "If your knee bends as you lift, the muscle is not ready — go back to quad sets."
order: 2
---

Lie on your back, bend your good leg with the foot flat, and keep the affected leg straight.

First tighten the front thigh muscle so the knee locks out completely.

Raise the straight leg about thirty centimetres from the floor, keeping the knee fully straight.

Hold three seconds, then lower slowly — the slow lowering is the part that actually builds strength.
