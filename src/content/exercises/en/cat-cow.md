title: "Cat–cow"
summary: "Moves the spine through its full range and eases morning stiffness."
bodyArea: "back"

sets: 2
reps: 10
hold: ""
equipment: ["Mat"]
cautions: "Move only within a comfortable range — never into pain."

Start on all fours: hands under shoulders, knees under hips.

As you breathe in: let your belly drop, lift your head and tailbone so your back arches downwards.

As you breathe out: round your back up towards the ceiling and drop your head between your arms.

Move slowly between the two positions with your breath. This is an excellent way to start the day if you wake with a stiff back.
