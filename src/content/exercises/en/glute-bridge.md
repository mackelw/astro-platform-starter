---
title: "Glute bridge"
summary: "Strengthens the gluteal muscles that protect both the low back and the knee."
bodyArea: "hip"
difficulty: "medium"
sets: 3
reps: 12
hold: "3s"
equipment: ["Mat"]
cautions: "If you feel it in the back of your thigh instead of the buttocks, bring your feet closer to you."
order: 1
---

Lie on your back with knees bent and feet flat, hip-width apart.

Squeeze your buttocks first, then lift your hips off the floor until your body forms a straight line from shoulder to knee.

Hold three seconds while keeping the squeeze, then lower slowly, one vertebra at a time.

Weak glutes are a common cause of both low back pain and knee pain, so this exercise helps both.
