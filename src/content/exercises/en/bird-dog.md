---
title: "Bird dog"
summary: "Builds trunk stability without loading the spine — a mainstay of back pain treatment."
bodyArea: "back"
difficulty: "medium"
sets: 3
reps: 8
hold: "5s"
equipment: ["Mat"]
cautions: "Do not let your pelvis tip; stability matters more than how high the limb goes."
order: 3
---

Start on all fours: hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat.

Extend your right arm forwards and your left leg backwards at the same time, until both are level with your back.

What matters is that your pelvis stays completely still, without tipping to either side — imagine a glass of water resting on your low back that must not spill.

Hold five seconds, return slowly, then swap sides.
