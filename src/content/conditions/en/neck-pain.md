The neck carries a head weighing around five kilograms, and every centimetre it tips forward multiplies the load on its muscles. That is why most neck pain relates to how you sit at a screen far more than to any injury.

In assessment we distinguish local muscular pain, pain referred from a facet joint, and radicular nerve pain running into the arm — the last needs a different approach and may warrant a medical opinion.

Improvement here does not come from sessions alone: changing your workstation and taking a short break every half hour make a bigger difference than any machine.
