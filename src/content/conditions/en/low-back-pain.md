Most low back pain is mechanical in origin: weak trunk muscles, prolonged sitting, and poor lifting technique. A disc herniation itself does not necessarily mean surgery — the large majority improve with consistent physiotherapy.

We start by establishing whether the pain comes from the disc, the facet joint or the muscle, because the treatment differs. Clinical tests and your own description of the pain usually settle this without imaging.

**When you need a doctor immediately.** If the pain comes with clear weakness in the foot, numbness around the saddle area, or loss of bladder or bowel control — these are red flags that call for urgent medical assessment, not physiotherapy sessions.
