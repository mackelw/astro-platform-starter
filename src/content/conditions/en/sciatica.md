Sciatica is a symptom, not a diagnosis: it is pain along the sciatic nerve, usually caused by pressure from a herniated disc, spinal canal narrowing, or a tight piriformis muscle.

The distinction matters because treatment differs: piriformis compression responds quickly to stretching and manual therapy, while disc compression needs a longer programme focused on trunk stability.

The great majority of cases settle within six to twelve weeks with conservative treatment and no surgery.

**Red flags needing a doctor immediately:** progressive weakness in the foot, numbness around the saddle area, or loss of bladder or bowel control.
