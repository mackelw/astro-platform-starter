The fracture heals in the cast, but the joint and the muscle pay for it: a few weeks of immobilisation are enough to stiffen the capsule and visibly waste the muscle.

We begin as soon as your orthopaedic surgeon clears removal of the fixation, and progress carefully — forceful early mobilisation can do harm, while delay leaves stiffness that is hard to treat later.

The goal is not simply to bend and straighten the joint, but to return function: to walk without a limp, climb stairs, and carry what you used to carry.

How long rehabilitation takes depends on the fracture site, how long it was immobilised, and your age. We give you a realistic estimate after the first assessment rather than a general promise.
