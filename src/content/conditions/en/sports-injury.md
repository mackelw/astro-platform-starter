A sports injury is not treated by rest alone. Prolonged rest weakens the muscle and leaves the joint unstable, which raises the chance of the injury recurring.

Ankle sprain is the most common one we see, and the commonest mistake is returning to play as soon as the pain goes: ligaments heal before joint position sense returns, and that is how sprains repeat.

We work in clear stages and gate the progression on criteria rather than the calendar: has range of motion returned? Does strength match the uninjured side? Can you pass balance and hop tests?

We also treat hamstring strains, shoulder injuries in throwing and swimming athletes, and knee pain in runners.
