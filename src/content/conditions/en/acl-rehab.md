ACL rehabilitation is the longest programme we run — usually six to nine months — and adherence to it decides whether you return to play.

Regaining full knee extension in the early weeks matters more than regaining flexion: a knee that does not straighten fully leaves a limp and lasting problems, and it is hard to recover later.

We do not clear return to sport on time alone: we measure strength on both sides and compare them, requiring at least ninety per cent symmetry along with passing hop tests before full return.

Returning to the pitch early is the leading cause of a second ACL tear, in the same knee or the other one. Patience here is not delay — it is part of the treatment.
