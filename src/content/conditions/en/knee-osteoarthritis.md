The grade of arthritis on an X-ray does not match the level of pain: many people have clear changes with little pain, and others the reverse. So we treat the patient, not the picture.

The strongest single factor in reducing pain is quadriceps strength — that muscle absorbs load away from the joint surface. One exercise done consistently outperforms any machine.

Movement is treatment, not a risk: complete rest increases stiffness and weakens the muscle, which increases pain. The aim is to modify activity, not stop it — level walking, swimming and a stationary bike all suit.

If the condition reaches a stage that limits your daily life despite consistent treatment, we will tell you plainly that joint replacement may be the better option and refer you to an orthopaedic surgeon.
